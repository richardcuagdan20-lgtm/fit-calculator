Best Car for Senior Citizens and PWD Family Members (PH)

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: The best car for senior citizens is the one they can get in and out of easily. For senior or PWD family members, look for a seat height that's easy to slide into, wide door openings, a low step-in, grab handles, cargo space for a wheelchair or walker, and an automatic transmission for easier driving. Test it with the actual person and their mobility aid before deciding.

Kapag may lolo, lola o family member with a disability, comfort and access matter as much as price. The best car is the one they can get in and out of with dignity. Here's what to check.

Getting in and out

  • Seat height: around hip height lets them slide in rather than climb or drop down
  • Door opening: wide openings make it easier with a cane or walker
  • Step-in height: a lower floor helps people with knee or hip pain
  • Grab handles: check where they are for the front and second rows

Space for mobility aids

  • Bring the actual wheelchair, walker or cane to the test drive
  • Try loading it with the seats in your usual setup
  • A 7-seater gives flexibility to fold seats when needed

For senior drivers

  • Automatic transmission for less effort in traffic
  • Good visibility and easy-to-read instruments
  • Reverse camera for parking (part of the new-car freebies)
  • Comfortable seat adjustment and steering position

Test-drive checklist

  1. Bring the person

    Let them try getting in and out of each row.

  2. Bring the aid

    Load and unload it yourself.

  3. Ride, don't just sit

    Check ride comfort over bumps.

  4. Check the seatbelt reach

    Easy to buckle without twisting.

Frequently asked questions

Which is easier for seniors to get into, a sedan or an MPV?

Many seniors find a seat at hip height easier, like an MPV, because they slide in rather than step down or climb up. Test with the person.

Will a folding wheelchair fit?

It depends on the wheelchair and the car. Bring it to the test drive and try loading it with the seats in your usual setup.

Is an automatic better for older drivers?

Often yes. It reduces effort in traffic and lets the driver focus on the road.
