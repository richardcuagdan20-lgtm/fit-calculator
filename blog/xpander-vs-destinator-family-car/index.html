Xpander vs Destinator: Which Family Car Fits?

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-21

Quick answer: Xpander vs Destinator, in short. Both seat seven. The Xpander is the more affordable, family-proven MPV with a 1.5L engine (105 PS), while the Destinator is a 7-seat SUV with a 1.5L turbo (163 PS, 250 Nm) and 214 mm ground clearance. Choose the Xpander for value and city use, the Destinator for more power on full loads and rougher roads.

Isang salita lang: Xpander is "maluwag," Destinator is "astig." But your family deserves more than one word. Here's a fair comparison to help you decide.

Size, seating and cargo

Both seat seven, so the question is how you'll use the space. The Xpander is a practical MPV built around Filipino families, easy to get in and out of. The Destinator is an SUV with a more commanding stance and 214 mm of ground clearance. Reviewers have praised the Destinator's cabin space. The best test: bring your family and the stuff you usually carry, and try the third row and the cargo area of both.

Engine, performance and fuel

The Destinator's turbo engine (163 PS, 250 Nm) is a clear step up when the car is full and you're climbing or overtaking on the expressway. The Xpander's 1.5L is simpler and adequate for city driving and regular family trips. One practical note from reviews: the Destinator has a 45-liter fuel tank, so plan fuel stops on long drives. Actual fuel economy depends a lot on traffic and driving style; see my fuel-saving tips.

Safety and features by variant

Features differ by variant: the Xpander comes in GLX and GLS trims, and the Destinator in GLX, GLS and GT. Higher variants generally add more comfort and driver-assist features. Ask me for the official spec sheet of the exact variants you're comparing so you're looking at the real list, not assumptions.

Which fits which family?

  • Mostly city, budget-conscious: Xpander. Lower starting price, easy to drive and park.
  • Frequent probinsya trips with a full car: Destinator. The turbo and torque make full-load highway driving easier.
  • Flood-prone areas or rough roads: both have good clearance; the Destinator's 214 mm adds margin.
  • Big family, growing kids: try both third rows in person before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Destinator bigger than the Xpander?

Both seat seven. The Destinator is an SUV with 214 mm ground clearance; compare cabin and cargo space in person on a test drive with your family.

Which is more powerful, Xpander or Destinator?

The Destinator. Its 1.5L turbo makes 163 PS and 250 Nm, versus the Xpander's 1.5L at 105 PS and 141 Nm.

Which is cheaper?

The Xpander, with an official SRP starting at ₱1,099,000. The Destinator starts at ₱1,389,000 (MMPC price list as of August 2026).

What transmission do they use?

The Xpander comes in a 5-speed manual or 4-speed automatic; the Destinator uses a CVT.
