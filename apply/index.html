Apply for financing

The fastest way to start is the budget calculator. It matches a unit to your income and sends your details to me so I can follow up.

Start with the calculator

The steps

  1. Check your fit

    Use the calculator to see which models fit your monthly budget and choose a down payment option.

  2. Talk with me

    I'll message or call you to confirm the unit, color and terms, and answer your questions.

  3. Submit documents

    Send the requirements for your situation. I'll check them before they go to the bank.

  4. Bank approval

    The bank evaluates your application. Approval time varies by bank and by how complete the documents are.

  5. Pay the down payment and release

    Once approved, settle the down payment (if any), sign the documents and drive home your Mitsubishi.

Typical documents

Banks set the final list, but most ask for the following:

  • Two valid government IDs with matching signatures
  • Proof of billing for your current address
  • Employed: certificate of employment with compensation and recent payslips or ITR
  • Business owner: DTI or SEC registration, business permit, bank statements and ITR or financial statements
  • OFW: employment contract, recent payslips or remittance records, and details of your co-borrower or attorney-in-fact in the Philippines
