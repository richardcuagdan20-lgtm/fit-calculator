Mitsubishi Xforce vs Xpander: Which One Suits You?

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-11-05

Quick answer: Choose the Xpander for seven seats and 225 mm ground clearance; choose the Xforce for a five-seat compact SUV with a CVT, drive modes and, on the GT, Mitsubishi Motors Safety Sensing (according to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines).

Choose the Xforce if you mostly carry up to five people and want an SUV look, a CVT and drive modes.

Choose the Xpander if you need seven seats or often drive through flood-prone streets.

Frequently asked questions

Which has more seats?

The Xpander seats seven; the Xforce seats five, according to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines.

Do they share an engine?

Both use a 1.5L MIVEC gasoline engine (4A91) with 105 PS and 141 Nm, according to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines. The Xforce uses a CVT; the Xpander a 4-speed AT or 5-speed MT.

Which has more ground clearance?

The Xpander has 225 mm and the Xforce 193 mm, according to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines.

Can I test drive both in SJDM?

Yes. Book with Richard in San Jose del Monte (SJDM) and ask to try both on the same visit.
