How downpayment and monthly work

Every financed car comes down to four numbers:

  • SRP: the suggested retail price of the unit.
  • Down payment (DP): what you pay upfront. On a Zero DP promo, none; a bigger DP means a smaller loan and a lower monthly.
  • Term: how long you pay. The promos here are computed on 5 years (60 months); shorter terms mean higher monthlies but less interest overall.
  • Monthly amortization: the fixed amount you pay the bank each month until the loan is done.

Compare all four together, not just the monthly. More on this in how car financing works and Zero DP vs low DP.

For a compact sedan like the Mirage G4, a down payment lowers the monthly noticeably, while Zero DP keeps your savings intact. Both are valid; it depends on whether cash on hand or a lower monthly matters more to you.

What all-in covers

  • LTO registration
  • TPL insurance
  • Comprehensive insurance for the first year
  • Chattel mortgage
  • Free PMS labor up to 5,000 km
  • Factory warranty

Freebies like tint, mats and a reverse camera usually come too. See what all-in includes.

Documents

  • Everyone: two valid government IDs with matching signatures, and proof of billing
  • Employed: certificate of employment with compensation, recent payslips or latest ITR
  • Business owner / self-employed: DTI or SEC registration, business or mayor's permit, latest ITR or financial statements, bank statements
  • OFW / seafarer: employment or crew contract, payslips or proof of remittance, and SPA or co-borrower details for the family member in the Philippines

Each bank can ask for more. Full list: car loan requirements. OFW? See the OFW car loan guide.

Income guide

A sensible guide: keep the monthly amortization at about 30–35% of your gross monthly income. Divide the monthly by 0.35 for a minimum income and by 0.30 for a comfortable one. A spouse or family member can sometimes join as a co-borrower so the bank considers combined income. The bank always makes the final call. Which variant

The Mirage G4 comes as the GLX (manual or CVT) and the GLS (CVT), all with the 1.2L gasoline engine. The CVT is easier in SJDM and EDSA traffic; the GLS adds more features. Frequently asked questions

How much is the Mirage G4 downpayment and monthly?

See the dated promo table on this page for the current all-in options on a 5-year term. For variants or DP amounts not listed, message Richard for the latest Mirage G4 promo.

Is there a Zero DP Mirage G4?

Zero DP depends on the month's promo and the variant. The current options are in the promo table; message Richard to confirm.

What salary do I need for a Mirage G4?

As a guide, keep the monthly at about 30–35% of your gross monthly income. Divide the monthly by 0.35 for a minimum income. The bank makes the final assessment.

What documents do I need for a Mirage G4 car loan?

Two valid IDs and proof of billing, plus income documents: COE and payslips or ITR if employed, business documents if self-employed, and contract and remittance proof if you're an OFW.

Can I pay the Mirage G4 in less than 5 years?

Yes, shorter terms are available. The monthly will be higher, but you pay less interest overall. Ask Richard for a computation.
