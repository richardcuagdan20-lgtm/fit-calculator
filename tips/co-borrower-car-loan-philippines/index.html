Co-Borrower Car Loan Philippines: How It Works

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: A co-borrower car loan in the Philippines lets two people, usually spouses or immediate family, apply together so the bank can consider combined income. The co-borrower shares responsibility for the loan and submits IDs and income documents. Each bank sets its own rules.

A co-borrower car loan in the Philippines lets two people apply together, so the bank can consider combined income. It's common for couples, families and OFWs. Here's how it works and what to think about first.

When a co-borrower helps

  • Your income alone is a bit short for the monthly
  • You're an OFW and need someone in the Philippines to sign
  • A couple wants to share the loan
  • You're newly employed and want a stronger application

Who can be a co-borrower

Banks commonly accept a spouse or immediate family member. Rules vary by bank, so ask before you prepare documents.

What the co-borrower needs

  • Valid IDs
  • Income documents (COE and payslips, or business documents)
  • Proof of billing

Full lists in car loan requirements.

Think it through first

  1. Shared responsibility

    The co-borrower shares the loan. Both should agree on the monthly and the term.

  2. Agree who pays

    Put it in writing between you.

  3. Check the budget together

    Use the 30–35% guide on combined gross income. Example: the Destinator GLX Zero DP at ₱28,615 a month fits about ₱81,800 to ₱95,400 combined (October 2026).

Co-borrower vs SPA for OFWs

An SPA lets someone sign on your behalf; a co-borrower shares the loan. See the OFW car loan guide and car loan approval tips.

Frequently asked questions

Who can be a co-borrower for a car loan?

Banks commonly accept a spouse or immediate family member. Each bank sets its own rules.

A co-borrower shares responsibility for the loan, so they should understand the monthly and the term before signing.

Yes. Typically IDs and income documents, similar to the main borrower.
