Dashcam Philippines Guide: Install, Use and Park Smart

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: Thinking of a dashcam? In the Philippines, many drivers now see it as basic protection. A dashcam gives you a record if there's an accident or dispute. Mount it behind the rearview mirror so it doesn't block your view, use a reliable memory card and check it regularly. For parking, choose well-lit, busy spots, reverse in when it's safe, fold your mirrors, and never leave valuables in sight.

Dashcams used to be for enthusiasts. Today, many Filipino drivers see them as basic protection. Combine one with smart parking habits and you avoid a lot of stress.

Why a dashcam helps

  • A record of what happened in an accident or road dispute
  • Helpful for insurance claims and police reports
  • Footage of hit-and-run or scrapes, with parking mode on some models
  • Peace of mind for family members driving your car

Choosing and installing

  1. Clear video, day and night

    Choose a model with good low-light recording and readable plate numbers.

  2. Mount behind the mirror

    So it doesn't block your view.

  3. Tidy, safe wiring

    Keep cables away from airbags and pedals. Have it installed properly.

  4. Right memory card

    Use a card made for continuous recording and check it monthly.

Smart parking habits

  • Choose well-lit, busy areas near guards or CCTV
  • Reverse in when it's safe, for an easier, safer exit
  • Fold your side mirrors in tight spaces
  • Park away from carts and door-swing zones when you can
  • Bags in the trunk, not on the seat; take valuables with you
  • Note your slot or take a photo of the pillar sign

Street and subdivision parking

Many LGUs and subdivisions have rules against street parking. Settle where your car will sleep before you buy, as I mention in the first-time buyer checklist.

After a parking-lot scrape

  1. Take photos

    The damage, the area and nearby cars.

  2. Ask for CCTV

    Talk to the guard or building management right away.

  3. Report and claim

    File a report if needed and call your insurer. See car insurance explained.

New cars come with a reverse camera among the freebies, which helps with tight parking.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I mount a dashcam?

Usually behind the rearview mirror, where it records the road without blocking your view. Keep wires tidy and away from airbags.

Does a dashcam need a special memory card?

Dashcams record continuously, so use a card rated for that and check or format it regularly as the dashcam manual advises.

Is it better to reverse into a parking slot?

Often yes, when it's safe and allowed. Driving out forward gives better visibility when leaving.

What do I do if my parked car gets scraped?

Take photos, check for a note, ask the guard or building management for CCTV, file a report if needed, and contact your insurer.
