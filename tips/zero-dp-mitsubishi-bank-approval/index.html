Zero DP Mitsubishi: What It Means and How Bank Approval Works

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-30

Quick answer: Zero DP Promo means the bank may finance the full unit price so you don't pay a down payment, with the main fees bundled in an all-in package. It is never automatic: the bank checks your income, credit record and documents first. On this site, a good result is shown as Pre-qualified, subject to bank approval.

"Zero DP" is the most searched promo term I see for Mitsubishi in Bulacan, and also the most misunderstood. It is real, and many of my clients drive home with it, but it works only when the bank says yes. This guide explains exactly what the Zero DP Promo is, what the bank checks, and what you can do to improve your chances.

What Zero DP means (and what it doesn't)

Zero DP means you do not pay a down payment on the unit. The bank finances the full unit price over the term (60 months on this month's promos). Because there is no DP, the loan is bigger and the monthly is higher than with a down payment.

It does not mean "automatic approval" or "no requirements". On the promos page, units with this option show Zero DP Promo as Option A, subject to bank approval, because the final DP depends on how the bank evaluates you.

How bank approval works, step by step

  1. Pre-qualification. You share your income type, monthly income and existing loans. The free AI Car Loan Application Assessment gives a result in minutes: at best, Pre-qualified, subject to bank approval.
  2. Application and documents. You fill out the application form and submit IDs, proof of income and proof of billing. See car loan requirements.
  3. Credit investigation. The bank checks your credit records and may call you, your employer or references.
  4. Bank decision. The bank may approve the loan as applied, approve with a down payment, or decline.
  5. Signing and release. Once the bank approves your loan, you sign the loan papers and we schedule the release.

Zero DP vs paying a down payment

Zero DP keeps your savings intact, which matters if you need an emergency fund. A down payment lowers the monthly and the total paid. Option B on the promos page shows a 30% DP version for every unit. For a deeper comparison, read Zero DP vs low DP and the Mitsubishi zero down payment promo guide.

Paano tumaas ang chance ma-approve

  • Choose a unit whose monthly fits the 30–35% guide.
  • Pay off or reduce small debts first.
  • Add a co-borrower (spouse or immediate family) if income is tight.
  • Submit complete documents the first time.
  • Keep your phone on for the verification call.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zero DP really zero cash out?

Zero DP means no down payment on the unit. Ask for the written breakdown of anything due at signing, such as the first monthly or reservation, because terms can vary per bank and month.

Why is Zero DP subject to bank approval?

Because the bank lends the full amount, it takes more risk, so it checks income, existing loans and credit history more closely. No seller can promise the result.

How long does bank approval take?

Usually a few working days after complete documents are submitted, longer if the bank needs more papers or verification. Incomplete documents are the main cause of delay.

What if the bank does not approve Zero DP?

Often the bank will offer approval with a down payment instead. Option B (30% DP) or a co-borrower are common alternatives.
