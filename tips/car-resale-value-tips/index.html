Car Resale Value Philippines: 8 Habits That Protect It

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: Car resale value in the Philippines comes down to habits. Resale value is protected by habits: follow PMS at an authorized service center and keep the records, avoid floods and fix damage properly, keep the interior and paint clean, skip extreme modifications, and keep all documents, keys and accessories. Popular models, variants and colors also tend to sell more easily.

The day you buy a car is also the day you start building its resale value. Hindi mo kailangang maging car expert. You just need a few consistent habits.

8 habits that protect resale value

  1. Follow the PMS schedule

    At an authorized service center, on time. See my PMS schedule guide.

  2. Keep every service record

    A complete folder of receipts is one of the strongest selling points.

  3. Avoid floods

    Flood history hurts value badly. Follow my flood rules.

  4. Repair damage properly

    Use proper body shops and insurance claims, and keep the paperwork.

  5. Protect paint and interior

    Regular washing, shade parking, seat covers and floor mats.

  6. Go easy on modifications

    Keep the original parts if you do modify.

  7. Keep mileage reasonable

    Not always possible, but well-documented mileage matters.

  8. Keep documents and extras complete

    OR/CR, spare key, manuals, tools and accessories.

Choices at purchase that help later

  • Popular models and variants tend to have more buyers
  • Common colors like white, silver and black are often easier to sell
  • The right transmission: automatics are in demand for city driving, manuals for work and budget buyers
  • Warranty and records: the 3-year warranty in the all-in package plus your PMS records reassure the next buyer

What hurts resale value most

  • Flood or major accident history
  • Missing service records
  • Smoke and pet odor inside the cabin
  • Extreme modifications
  • Incomplete documents or unsettled violations

When it's time to sell

Clean it well, gather every record and accessory, and compare a trade-in with selling privately. My car trade-in tips walk you through the process.

Frequently asked questions

Does PMS at an authorized service center affect resale value?

Complete, documented service history gives buyers and appraisers confidence and supports a better price.

Do modifications increase resale value?

Usually not. Extreme modifications can narrow your buyers. Keep original parts if you modify, so you can restore the car.

Does car color affect resale value?

Common colors like white, silver and black are often easier to sell because more buyers want them, but demand varies by model.

Does flood damage affect resale value?

Yes, significantly. Avoid floods, and if the car is submerged, have it towed and inspected and keep the repair records.
