Best 7-Seater for Filipino Families in 2026

By Richard Cuagdan, Car Sales Executive · 2026-10-21

Seven seats is the magic number for many Filipino households: parents, kids, a lola or yaya, and room for pasalubong. Mitsubishi has four 7-seaters in the current lineup, and each fits a different kind of family.

For the tightest budget: Xpander

With an SRP starting at ₱1,099,000, the Xpander is the most affordable way into a Mitsubishi 7-seater. Its high ground clearance is a real advantage during the rainy season.

For style with practicality: Xpander Cross

The Xpander Cross keeps the Xpander's space and adds a crossover look and a more upscale interior, at an SRP of ₱1,378,000.

For more power and tech: Destinator

The Destinator's turbocharged engine makes 163 PS, a big step up for families who regularly fill every seat and head north or south on the expressway.

For long drives and rough roads: Montero Sport

If you travel to the province often or need a diesel with real towing and off-road ability, the Montero Sport and its Black Series variants are built for it.
