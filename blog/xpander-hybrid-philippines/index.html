Xpander Hybrid Philippines: What We Know | SJDM, San Jose del Monte

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-11-16

Quick answer: The Xpander Hybrid (Xpander HEV and Xpander Cross HEV) has been on sale in Thailand since February 1, 2024. Mitsubishi Motors Philippines has not announced a local launch date or SRP, so it is not available in the Philippines yet.

Many of you in San Jose del Monte (SJDM) and around Bulacan have asked me about the Xpander Hybrid. Mitsubishi calls it the Xpander HEV (hybrid electric vehicle), and there is also an Xpander Cross HEV. This page puts what Mitsubishi Motors has officially announced in one place: where it is sold, the key specs, its official Thai prices converted to pesos for reference, and how it compares with the Xpander we sell here today.

Not available in the Philippines yet: Mitsubishi Motors Philippines (MMPC) has not announced a local launch date, variants or SRP for the Xpander Hybrid. It is not in Philippine showrooms, so I can't quote a local price or take reservations for it yet. I'll update this page when MMPC announces local details.

What is the Xpander Hybrid?

According to Mitsubishi Motors, the Xpander HEV and Xpander Cross HEV are full hybrid versions of the Xpander seven-seater. They don't need charging from a plug: a 1.6L gasoline engine and a generator make electricity, and an electric motor drives the front wheels. Mitsubishi Motors says the system was newly developed from its plug-in hybrid (PHEV) technology.

The car runs on the battery alone when starting off and at low speeds. It switches to hybrid driving when climbing or accelerating, the engine helps at high speeds, and braking recovers energy back into the battery. Mitsubishi Motors put the drive battery under the front seats, so the three-row, seven-seat cabin is kept.

Fun facts about the Xpander Hybrid

  • Built from plug-in hybrid know-how. Mitsubishi Motors says the HEV system was newly developed from its PHEV technology, the same family as the Outlander PHEV.
  • A quiet mode for early mornings. EV Priority mode runs on the battery without starting the engine, which Mitsubishi Motors pitches for quiet drives through residential areas early in the morning. If the battery is low, Charge mode tops it up, whether moving or stopped.
  • 255 Nm from the electric motor. That torque arrives quickly from a standstill, which Mitsubishi Motors says helps with merging and U-turns in city traffic.
  • A Mitsubishi first under the hood. The 1.6L engine uses the first electric water pump ever fitted to a Mitsubishi Motors engine, to cut mechanical losses.
  • Better city fuel consumption. According to Mitsubishi Motors, it's about 34% better in urban driving and about 15% better combined than its gasoline CVT model, on the NEDC lab test.
  • Seven seats kept. The drive battery sits under the front seats, so the hybrid keeps the Xpander's three rows.
  • Made in Thailand. The Xpander HEV and Xpander Cross HEV are built at Mitsubishi Motors' Laem Chabang Plant in Thailand.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Xpander Hybrid available in the Philippines?

Not yet. Mitsubishi Motors Philippines (MMPC) sells the Xpander and Xpander Cross with a 1.5L gasoline engine. The hybrid versions are sold in Thailand, and MMPC has not announced a Philippine launch.

When will the Xpander Hybrid come to the Philippines?

There is no announced date. In April 2026, Mitsubishi Motors said MMPC plans to start building a new hybrid model at its Santa Rosa, Laguna plant around mid-2028, subject to approval under the government's EVIS program. Mitsubishi Motors has not said which model it will be.

How much is the Xpander Hybrid in the Philippines?

There is no Philippine SRP. In Thailand, the official price is THB 935,000 for the Xpander HEV and THB 953,000 for the Xpander Cross HEV. That is about ₱1,755,000 and ₱1,789,000 at the BSP reference rate of November 12, 2026, for reference only. A future Philippine price could be quite different.

What engine does the Xpander HEV have?

According to Mitsubishi Motors Thailand, a 1.6L MIVEC gasoline engine (95 PS, 134 Nm) works with an 85 kW (116 PS) electric motor with 255 Nm of torque and a lithium-ion hybrid battery, driving the front wheels.

How fuel efficient is the Xpander Hybrid?

According to Mitsubishi Motors, fuel consumption is about 34% better in urban driving and about 15% better in combined driving than its gasoline CVT model, on the NEDC lab test. Real-world results depend on how and where you drive.

Can I reserve an Xpander Hybrid with Richard now?

Not yet, because there are no Philippine variants or prices to reserve. Message me and I will update you when MMPC announces local details. If you need a seven-seater now, the current Xpander and Xpander Cross are available.
