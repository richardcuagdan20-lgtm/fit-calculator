13th Month Pay 2026: A Smart Car Budget Plan

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-21

Quick answer: Your 13th month pay is one-twelfth of the basic salary you earned in the year, and employers must release it by December 24. Before it disappears in holiday sales, split it: emergency fund first, then Christmas, then a car fund and ownership costs like PMS and insurance.

Dumating na ang 13th month… and by December 15, naubos na sa sale? Hindi ka nag-iisa. A little plan turns your 13th month into something that lasts longer than a shopping cart.

How 13th month pay is computed and when it's due

Under Philippine law, rank-and-file employees are entitled to 13th month pay equal to one-twelfth of the total basic salary earned within the calendar year. Employers must release it on or before December 24. If you started mid-year, it's prorated based on what you actually earned.

A simple split framework

There's no single right formula, but here's a starting point many families use. Adjust it to your situation:

  • Emergency fund first: if you don't have a few months of expenses saved, prioritize this.
  • Debts: pay down high-interest credit card balances.
  • Pasko: set a fixed amount for gifts, Noche Buena and family, and stick to it.
  • Car fund: if a car is your next goal, put a portion aside for reservation, initial costs or your first year of ownership.
  • Maintenance: if you already own a car, set aside for PMS, tires, battery and insurance renewal.

Car fund vs impulse spending

Online sales are designed to make you buy now. Before you check out, ask: "Will I still care about this in March?" A car fund works best in a separate account you don't touch, so the money is there when you're ready.

Hidden ownership costs to budget for

  • Fuel (see my fuel-saving tips)
  • Comprehensive insurance renewal after the first year
  • PMS at the intervals in your owner's manual
  • Parking and tolls
  • Tires and battery replacement over time

The goal is a monthly that still leaves room for savings, not one that sasakal sa'yo every payday.

Frequently asked questions

How is 13th month pay computed in the Philippines?

It's the total basic salary you earned during the calendar year divided by 12. Allowances and overtime are generally not included in the basic salary.

When is the deadline for 13th month pay?

Employers must release it on or before December 24.

Can I use my 13th month pay for a car?

Yes, it can go toward a car fund or reservation, but keep an emergency fund first and budget for fuel, insurance and maintenance.

Should I buy a car in December?

December can be a good time to ask about year-end promos, but buy only if the monthly and ownership costs fit your budget for the full term.
