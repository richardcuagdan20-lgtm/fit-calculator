Xpander vs Xpander Cross vs Destinator: Which 7-Seater?

By Richard Cuagdan, Car Sales Executive · 2026-10-21

All three seat seven and are popular with families in Bulacan. The right choice depends on your budget, how often you travel far, and how much power and tech you want.

Choose the Xpander if…

You want the most affordable 7-seater, mostly drive around town and on weekend trips, and value practicality over extras. The GLS AT adds a 10-inch display with wireless smartphone connectivity and a Multi-Around Monitor. See Xpander promos.

Choose the Xpander Cross if…

You like the Xpander's packaging but want a tougher crossover look and a nicer cabin, including two-tone synthetic leather with Heat Guard. See Xpander Cross promos.

Choose the Destinator if…

You often carry a full load or drive long distances and want noticeably more power from the turbo engine. Higher variants add more technology, and the GT adds Forward Collision Mitigation and a panoramic sunroof. See Destinator promos.

Still undecided?

Book a back-to-back test drive. Driving them on the same day usually makes the choice obvious.
