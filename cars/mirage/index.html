Frequently asked questions

How much is the Mirage 2026?

SRP ₱720,000 (GLX M/T) or ₱781,000 (GLX CVT), Oct 2026 list, November update pending.

What is the Mirage low DP?

GLX M/T: ₱18,000 DP and ₱14,833 a month; GLX CVT: ₱23,000 DP and ₱16,090 a month (60 months, all-in). Pre-qualified, subject to bank approval.

What engine does the Mirage have?

According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines, a 1.2L 3-cylinder DOHC MIVEC engine (3A92) with a CVT or 5-speed manual.

Is the Mirage easy to park?

According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines, it has a 4.6-meter turning radius and seats up to five adults.

Mirage or Mirage G4?

The Mirage is the hatchback; the Mirage G4 is the sedan with a separate trunk. Compare both prices on the price list.

Can I test drive a Mirage in SJDM?

Yes. Book online and Richard will confirm in San Jose del Monte (SJDM).
