How the computation works

  1. Amount financed

    SRP minus your down payment (DP).

  2. Add-on interest

    The bank applies an add-on rate that depends on the term; longer terms carry a higher total rate.

  3. Monthly

    Amount financed plus interest, divided by the number of months. Promo packages may bundle registration and insurance (all-in).

Frequently asked questions

How do I compute a brand new car installment in the Philippines?

Subtract the down payment from the SRP, apply the bank's add-on rate for your term, then divide by the number of months. The calculator does this for you; figures are estimates.

What is a typical car loan term?

Common terms run from 1 to 5 years. A longer term lowers the monthly but raises the total you pay.

Is the monthly from the calculator final?

No. It is an estimate. Your bank sets the final rate and terms; results are Pre-qualified, subject to bank approval.

What do I need to apply for a car loan?

Valid IDs, proof of billing and proof of income. See the car loan requirements guide for the full list per employment type.

How do I send my computation to Richard?

After computing, fill in the short form under your result, or message Richard on Messenger with your model, down payment and term.
