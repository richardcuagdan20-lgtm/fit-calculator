Montero Sport vs Destinator: Which Family SUV?

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: Montero Sport vs Destinator, in short. Both seat seven. The Montero Sport is a ladder-frame SUV with a 2.4L turbo diesel (181 PS, 430 Nm), available in 4WD on the Black Series, built for long drives, heavy loads and rough roads. The Destinator has a 1.5L turbo gasoline engine (163 PS, 250 Nm), a CVT and 214 mm clearance, and a lower entry price. Destinator GLX starts at ₱1,389,000; Montero Sport GLX MT at ₱1,568,000.

Two 7-seat SUVs, two different personalities. The Montero Sport is the tough, long-distance diesel. The Destinator is the newer turbo-gas family SUV. Here's how to choose.

Frequently asked questions

Which is more powerful?

The Montero Sport has more torque: 430 Nm from its 2.4L diesel versus 250 Nm from the Destinator's 1.5L turbo. Power is 181 PS vs 163 PS.

Which one has 4WD?

The Montero Sport Black Series 4WD A/T. The Destinator is 2WD.

Which is cheaper per month?

The Destinator GLX from ₱26,827 a month with ₱93K DP, versus the Montero Sport GLX MT from ₱28,265 with ₱30K DP (October 2026, 60 months, all-in).

Which is better for the city?

The Destinator's CVT and smaller turbo engine suit city traffic well. The Montero Sport GLS and Black Series have an 8-speed automatic for those who want the diesel.
