Frequently asked questions

How much is the L300 2026?

L300 Cab and Chassis ₱829,000; L300 Dual AC ₱1,039,000 (Oct 2026 list, November update pending).

What is the L300 low DP?

Cab and Chassis: ₱133,000 DP and ₱14,944 a month; Dual AC: ₱163,000 DP and ₱18,729 a month (60 months, all-in). Pre-qualified, subject to bank approval.

What engine does the L300 have?

According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines, a 2.2L Euro 4 turbo diesel (4N14) with 99.25 PS and 200 Nm, and a 5-speed manual.

Can a business owner finance an L300?

Yes. Banks usually ask for business registration, permits, ITR or financial statements and bank statements.

Can I get a custom body on the Cab and Chassis?

Yes, the Cab and Chassis is meant for a body built to your business. Ask Richard about body options and timelines.

Can I test drive an L300 in SJDM?

Yes. Book a test drive with Richard in San Jose del Monte (SJDM).
