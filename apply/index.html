What happens next

  1. I review your details

    I check which unit and payment option fit what you shared.

  2. We talk

    I'll message or call you to confirm the unit, color and terms and share this month's promo.

  3. Submit documents

    For financing, send the requirements for your situation. I'll check them before they go to the bank.

  4. Approval and release

    Once the bank approves (or once payment is settled for cash), we schedule your release.

Typical documents for financing

  • Two valid government IDs with matching signatures
  • Proof of billing
  • Employed: certificate of employment with compensation, payslips or ITR
  • Business owner: DTI or SEC registration, business permit, bank statements, ITR or financial statements
  • OFW: employment contract, payslips or remittance records, and co-borrower or attorney-in-fact details
