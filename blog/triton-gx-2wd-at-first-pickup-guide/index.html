Triton GX 2.4D 2WD A/T: First-Time Pickup Guide

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: A pickup is longer, sits higher and rides firmer when empty than a sedan or MPV, so give yourself time to learn its size, turning and parking. The Triton GX 2.4D 2WD A/T is an automatic, which makes the switch easier. In October 2026: SRP ₱1,430,000, Zero DP ₱28,900 a month or ₱140K DP ₱25,200, with ₱20K fuel.

Unang pickup mo? Congrats, it's one of the most useful vehicles you can own. But it drives differently from a sedan or MPV. Here's what to expect with the Triton GX AT.

What's different about a pickup

  • Longer body: give more room when turning and parking
  • Higher seating: better view ahead, but check blind spots and the area close to the bumpers
  • Firmer ride when empty: the rear is built to carry loads
  • Diesel torque: strong pulling power for loads and climbs

Why the GX AT suits first-timers

  • Automatic: one less thing to learn in traffic
  • 2.4L 4N16 intercooled turbo diesel
  • 2WD, which suits paved and provincial roads
  • Double cab with rear seat for family use

Driving and parking tips

  1. Practice in an open lot

    Learn the turning circle and where the corners are.

  2. Take wider turns

    Watch the rear wheels in tight corners.

  3. Use mirrors and the reverse camera

    A reverse camera is among the new-car freebies; still turn your head.

  4. Check height limits

    Some parking buildings have low clearance.

Cargo bed basics

  • Stay within the rated payload for your variant
  • Tie down loads with straps or a net
  • Cover rain-sensitive cargo with a tarpaulin
  • Don't let passengers ride in the bed

Frequently asked questions

Is a pickup hard to drive?

Not with an automatic and some practice. The main adjustment is its length and turning circle in tight streets and parking.

Why does a pickup ride firmer when empty?

Its rear suspension is built to carry loads, so it can feel firmer with an empty bed.

Can a pickup be a family car?

The Triton is a double cab with a rear seat, so it can carry the family. Try the rear seat during the test drive.

Is the Triton GX automatic?

Yes, the Triton GX 2.4D 2WD A/T is an automatic with the 2.4L turbo diesel.
