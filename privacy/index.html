Who we are

This website, its Budget Fit Calculator and our Facebook and Messenger lead forms are run by Richard Dennis Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive at Mitsubishi San Juan Motors, San Jose del Monte (SJDM), Bulacan. He is responsible for the personal information you share with us.

Information we collect

  • Your name
  • Your contact number

How we use it

We use your information only to send you vehicle quotations, financing options and Mitsubishi promos that you asked about, and to follow up on your inquiry. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the dealership and its partner financing institutions when you ask us to process a quotation or loan application.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this site. These tools collect anonymous behavioral data such as pages viewed, clicks and scrolling. Text typed into forms is masked and not recorded by Clarity.

Spam protection

To protect our forms from spam and automated abuse, we use simple checks in your browser (such as a hidden field and submission timing) and may use Cloudflare Turnstile, a service by Cloudflare, Inc. Turnstile may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and device characteristics to tell people from bots. It runs invisibly for most visitors and does not use advertising cookies. Turnstile is governed by Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum; see also Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

How we keep it

Your information is stored securely, can be accessed only by authorized dealership staff, and is kept only as long as needed for your inquiry or as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed, to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

Contact us or request deletion

To ask about your data, correct it, or have it deleted, message Richard Dennis Cuagdan through our Facebook Page or in Messenger, or visit Mitsubishi San Juan Motors in San Jose del Monte, Bulacan (Monday to Saturday, 8am to 5pm).
