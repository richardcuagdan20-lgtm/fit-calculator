Mitsubishi Mirage G4 Price Philippines 2026

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-11-05

Quick answer: The Mitsubishi Mirage G4 price in the Philippines is ₱793,000 for the GLX M/T, ₱841,000 for the GLX CVT and ₱937,000 for the GLS CVT (Oct 2026 list, November update pending).

GLX or GLS?

According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines, every Mirage G4 uses the 1.2L MIVEC engine (78 PS, 100 Nm) and comes with driver and passenger airbags, ABS with EBD, ASC, Hill Start Assist and ISO-Fix. The GLX is offered as a manual or CVT; the GLS is CVT only. Ask Richard to walk you through the trim differences in person.
