Brand New vs Second Hand Car Philippines

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: Brand new vs second hand car in the Philippines: a second-hand car usually costs less upfront, but a brand-new car gives a known history, a 3-year warranty and an all-in package with registration and insurance, plus Zero DP options.

Brand new vs second hand car in the Philippines: the second-hand price looks cheaper, but the full picture includes warranty, history, repairs and financing. Here's a fair comparison.

Side by side

Brand newSecond hand
HistoryKnown: you're the first ownerUnknown unless documented
Warranty3-year warranty in the all-in packageUsually short or none
Registration and insuranceIncluded in all-in packageYour cost
Flood or accident riskNone at releaseNeeds inspection
Down paymentZero DP promos availableDepends on the seller

When brand new makes sense

  • You want predictable costs and a warranty
  • You'll keep the car many years
  • Zero DP lets you keep your savings: see the Zero DP list

When second hand can make sense

  • You're paying cash on a tight budget
  • You can have it inspected by a trusted mechanic
  • Documents are complete and history is clear

Second-hand checklist

  1. Documents

    OR/CR, deed of sale, IDs; no unsettled loan.

  2. Flood and accident signs

    Rust, odor, water marks, uneven panels.

  3. Service records

    Proof of regular PMS.

  4. Mechanic inspection

    Before paying anything.

Frequently asked questions

Is a second-hand car cheaper?

Usually the price is lower, but add repairs, unknown history and shorter or no warranty when you compare.

What do I get with a brand-new car?

A full warranty (3 years in the all-in package), known history, and an all-in package with registration and insurance.

What should I check on a second-hand car?

Documents (OR/CR, deed of sale), flood or accident history, service records, and a mechanic's inspection.

Can I finance a brand-new car with zero down?

Yes, through Zero DP all-in promos, subject to bank approval. Example: Xpander GLX MT at ₱22,641 a month (October 2026).
