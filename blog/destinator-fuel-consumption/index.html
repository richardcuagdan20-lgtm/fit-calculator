Destinator Fuel Consumption: Engine Facts and Saving Tips

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-11-05

Quick answer: Mitsubishi Motors Philippines does not publish an official km/L figure for the Destinator. Fuel use depends on traffic, load, speed and driving style; a test drive with the trip computer gives you a real-world feel.

Fuel cost is a fair question before buying a 7-seater. Here is what we know about the Destinator engine and what affects how much fuel you use. Get a Destinator quote on the variants page.

Engine facts

  • 1.5L 4B40 turbocharged gasoline engine, Euro 4
  • 163 PS and 250 N-m
  • CVT, front-wheel drive
  • 45 L fuel tank, RON 95 and over

According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines. No official km/L figure is published, so this page does not quote one.

What affects fuel consumption

  • Traffic: stop-and-go city traffic uses more fuel than steady NLEX driving
  • Load: seven passengers and luggage add weight
  • Speed: steady, moderate speeds use less fuel
  • Tires and maintenance: correct tire pressure and on-time PMS help
  • Aircon and idling: long idling with the AC on adds up

Saving tips

Accelerate smoothly, anticipate stops, and plan trips outside peak hours. More in fuel saving tips.

See it on the trip computer

Frequently asked questions

What is the official Destinator km/L?

Mitsubishi Motors Philippines does not publish an official fuel economy figure for the Destinator, so this page does not quote one.

What fuel does the Destinator use?

Gasoline, RON 95 and over, according to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines. The tank holds 45 L.

Does the turbo use more fuel?

It depends on how you drive. Smooth, steady driving uses less fuel; hard acceleration uses more.
