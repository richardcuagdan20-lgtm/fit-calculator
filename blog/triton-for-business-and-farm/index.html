Mitsubishi Triton for Business and Farm Use (2026)

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: The Triton for business and farm use, in short. The Mitsubishi Triton is a 2.4L turbo-diesel pickup with a cargo bed, offered in 2WD and 4WD and in manual or automatic by variant. It suits contractors, farms, resorts and businesses that haul supplies on weekdays and carry the family on weekends. In October 2026, the Triton GX AT is ₱1,430,000 SRP, from ₱28,900 a month at Zero DP or ₱25,200 a month with ₱140K DP, plus ₱20K fuel.

Sa probinsya at sa negosyo, a pickup does two jobs. Monday to Saturday it carries sacks of feed, hollow blocks, tools or resort supplies. On Sunday it takes the family to church and lunch. That's the job the Mitsubishi Triton was built for.

Triton at a glance

  • Engine: 2.4L 4N16 intercooled turbo diesel
  • Transmission: 6-speed manual or automatic, by variant
  • Drivetrain: 2WD or 4WD, by variant
  • Body: pickup with an open cargo bed

The lineup runs from the work-focused GL variants up to the GLX, GLS and Athlete. Exact features differ per variant, so ask me for the official spec sheet of the ones you're comparing.

Who the Triton is for

  • Contractors and hardware owners: tools, materials and crew in one trip.
  • Farm owners: feed, fertilizer, harvest and farm roads that turn muddy after rain.
  • Resort and events businesses: supplies, coolers and equipment.
  • Families in the province: weekend family use with a cargo bed for balikbayan boxes and pasalubong.

2WD or 4WD?

This is the biggest choice, and it depends on your roads, not your pride.

  • 2WD is enough for paved highways, city deliveries and well-kept provincial roads. It's usually the more affordable choice.
  • 4WD earns its keep on mud, steep unpaved farm roads, river crossings and hilly sites, especially during the rainy season.

Ask yourself: how many days a year do I really need 4WD? If the answer is "every rainy season, every week," get 4WD.

Cargo bed tips for working owners

  1. Stay within the rated payload

    Overloading wears the suspension, brakes and tires faster and is a safety risk. Check the official spec for your variant.

  2. Tie down every load

    Use straps or a cargo net so sacks and tools don't shift on bumps.

  3. Protect the bed

    A bed liner helps against scratches from tools and hollow blocks. Ask what accessories are available.

  4. Cover what needs cover

    Feed, cement and electronics need protection from rain. Keep a tarpaulin in the cab.

Frequently asked questions

Should I get a 2WD or 4WD Triton?

Choose 2WD if your routes are paved or well-maintained provincial roads. Choose 4WD if you regularly drive on mud, steep farm roads, river crossings or unpaved hills.

How much is the Triton GX AT promo in October 2026?

SRP ₱1,430,000. Zero DP at ₱28,900 a month or ₱140K DP at ₱25,200 a month on 60 months, all-in, with ₱20K fuel. Subject to bank approval.

Can a business finance a Triton?

Yes. Self-employed buyers and businesses commonly submit business registration, ITR or financial statements and bank statements. Approval is subject to bank evaluation.

Is a pickup comfortable for the family?

The Triton is a double-cab pickup with a rear seat, so it can do family duty on weekends. Bring your family to the test drive to check the rear seat.
