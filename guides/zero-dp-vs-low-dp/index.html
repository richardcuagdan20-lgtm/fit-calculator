Zero DP vs Low DP: Which Car Promo Is Right for You?

By Richard Cuagdan, Car Sales Executive · 2026-10-21

Many promos give you two choices on the same unit: Zero DP, where you pay no down payment, or a low-DP option where you pay a modest amount upfront in exchange for a lower monthly.

A real example

Using the October 2026 promo for the Xpander GLX AT on 60 months:

OptionUpfrontMonthly
Zero DP₱0₱23,877
Low DP₱138,000₱20,892

The low-DP option saves about ₱2,985 a month. Over 60 months that adds up to roughly ₱179,100, which is more than the ₱138,000 paid upfront. In this example, paying the down payment costs less overall.

Promo valid for October 2026, subject to bank approval and change without notice.

When Zero DP makes sense

  • You need a car now but want to keep your savings intact as an emergency fund.
  • Your monthly income comfortably covers the higher amortization.
  • You'd rather put spare cash into your business or other priorities.

When low DP makes sense

  • You have savings set aside and still keep an emergency buffer after paying the DP.
  • A lower monthly gives you more breathing room for fuel and maintenance.
  • You want to pay less in total over the life of the loan.

How to decide

Start with the monthly you can sustain for five years, not just this year. Then ask whether the down payment would leave you without a safety net. If both options fit, the low-DP option usually costs less overall. The calculator shows both side by side for your budget.
