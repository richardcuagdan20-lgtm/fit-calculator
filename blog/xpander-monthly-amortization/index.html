Xpander Monthly Amortization 2026: All Variants

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: The Xpander monthly amortization for October 2026 (60 months, all-in) starts at ₱22,641 at Zero DP for the GLX MT, ₱23,877 for the GLX AT, ₱25,937 for the GLS AT and ₱28,389 for the Xpander Cross. With a down payment, the GLX MT goes down to ₱19,811 a month.

Here's the Xpander monthly amortization for every variant in October 2026, on a 5-year (60-month) all-in term, plus the total you'll pay and the income each one needs.

Income you'll need

At 30–35% of gross monthly income for the Zero DP monthly:

  • GLX M/T: ₱64,700 – ₱75,500
  • GLX A/T: ₱68,200 – ₱79,600
  • GLS A/T: ₱74,100 – ₱86,500
  • Xpander Cross: ₱81,100 – ₱94,600

Zero DP or low DP?

Paying the down payment lowers both the monthly and the total of payments. Zero DP keeps your cash for emergencies. See Zero DP vs low DP and the variant breakdown in Xpander GLX vs GLS vs Cross.
