Road Trip from Bulacan: Baguio, Zambales, Batangas

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: Planning a road trip from Bulacan? From Bulacan, Baguio is usually reached via NLEX, SCTEX and TPLEX, then a mountain road up; Zambales via NLEX and SCTEX toward Subic; and Batangas via NLEX, Skyway, SLEX and STAR Tollway. Load your RFID for each operator, plan rest stops, and prepare the car for mountain climbs and beach sand.

Weekend na! Bulacan is a great starting point for road trips: north to the mountains, west to the beaches, or south to Batangas. Here's how to plan each, plus the prep that keeps the trip fun.

Routes below are typical; always check a live navigation app and toll operator advisories before leaving.

Baguio: mountain drive

  • Typical route: NLEX, then SCTEX and TPLEX, then a mountain road up to Baguio
  • Going up: steady throttle, don't tailgate on climbs
  • Going down: use a lower gear (or the low range on an automatic) instead of riding the brakes
  • No overtaking on blind curves; watch for fog and rain
  • Bring jackets, it's cold up there

Zambales: beach drive

  • Typical route: NLEX, then SCTEX toward Subic, then the coastal road
  • Don't drive onto soft sand unless you know it's firm
  • Rinse the car, especially the underside, after salty air and sand
  • Pack shade, water and sunblock

Batangas: south trip

  • Typical route: NLEX to the Skyway, then SLEX and STAR Tollway
  • Crossing Metro Manila: time it outside peak hours
  • Check the RFID systems your route needs, and load them

Frequently asked questions

What's the usual route from Bulacan to Baguio?

Many drivers take NLEX, then SCTEX and TPLEX, then a mountain road up to Baguio. Check traffic and road advisories before leaving.

How do I drive safely on mountain roads?

Use a lower gear going down instead of riding the brakes, don't overtake on blind curves, and keep to your lane.

What's the route to Batangas from Bulacan?

Commonly NLEX to the Skyway, then SLEX and STAR Tollway into Batangas. Routes depend on your exact destination.

Do I need different RFID for different expressways?

Different operators use different RFID systems, so check that you have and have loaded the ones your route needs.
