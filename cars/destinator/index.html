Frequently asked questions

How much is the Destinator?

SRP from ₱1,389,000 for the GLX up to ₱1,939,000 for the GT two-tone (Oct 2026 list, November update pending).

What engine does the Destinator have?

According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines, a 1.5L turbocharged gasoline engine (4B40) with 163 PS and 250 N-m, paired with a CVT.

How many seats does the Destinator have?

Seven, in three rows, according to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines.

Is the Destinator 4x4?

No. According to Mitsubishi Motors Philippines it is 2WD (front-wheel drive), with five drive modes including Gravel and Mud.

What is the Destinator ground clearance?

214 mm, which Mitsubishi Motors Philippines describes as best-in-class.

What is the lowest Destinator down payment?

See the variant table on this page for the low DP and sample monthly per variant. Pre-qualified, subject to bank approval.

Can I test drive the Destinator in SJDM?

Yes. Book online and Richard will confirm your schedule in San Jose del Monte.
