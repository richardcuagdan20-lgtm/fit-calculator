What "zero DP all-in" means

No down payment, and the package covers LTO registration, TPL insurance, 1-year comprehensive insurance with the rate locked for 3 years, chattel mortgage, free labor up to 5,000 km and a 3-year warranty. Ask for the written breakdown of anything due at signing. More in what all-in includes.

Pros and cons

  • Pro: keep your savings for emergencies or business
  • Pro: drive sooner
  • Con: higher monthly and total of payments than a low DP option
  • Con: needs a bit more income to qualify

Which Mitsubishi has the lowest zero down payment monthly?
