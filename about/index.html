About Richard Dennis Cuagdan

Car Sales Executive, Mitsubishi San Juan Motors, San Jose del Monte, Bulacan

I'm Richard Dennis Cuagdan, a Car Sales Executive at Mitsubishi San Juan Motors, an authorized Mitsubishi dealership in San Jose del Monte, Bulacan.

Most of the people I work with fall into three groups: employed buyers looking for a monthly that fits their salary, business owners who need a dependable vehicle for work, and OFWs and their families who are buying from abroad or for loved ones at home. Each situation has different paperwork and different priorities, and I take time to understand yours before recommending a unit.

How I work

  • I explain the SRP, down payment and monthly together so you can decide with complete numbers.
  • I tell you which documents your bank application needs and help you complete them.
  • I stay your single point of contact from inquiry to release.
  • I'm reachable on Messenger, including for clients in other time zones.
