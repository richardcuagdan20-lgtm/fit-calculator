What Does 'All-In' Include in a Car Promo?

By Richard Cuagdan, Car Sales Executive · 2026-10-21

"All-in" means the usual extra charges of buying a car are already part of the package, so the down payment you see is what you pay upfront. Here is what the current promos at Mitsubishi San Juan Motors include.

Included in the all-in package

  • 3 years LTO registration – your plates and registration are handled for the first three years.
  • 3 years TPL insurance – the compulsory third-party liability insurance required for registration.
  • 1 year comprehensive insurance, rate locked for 3 years – covers damage and theft; the rate is locked for the following renewals within three years.
  • Chattel mortgage – the fee to register the bank's lien on the car.
  • Free labor up to 5,000 km – labor for the early periodic maintenance service.
  • 3 years warranty – manufacturer warranty coverage, subject to its terms.

Freebies

Current promos also come with full tint, floor mats, a seat cover (for fabric seats), a reverse camera, an early warning device, basic tools, an umbrella, a microfiber cloth and a keychain. Many variants also come with a fuel freebie; ask me for the details on the unit you like.

What's not included

Fuel beyond the freebie, toll fees, parts and fluids for maintenance, and renewal of comprehensive insurance after the first year are still on you. Planning for these keeps ownership stress-free.

Promo valid for October 2026, subject to bank approval and change without notice.
