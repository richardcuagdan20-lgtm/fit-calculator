Trade In Car Philippines: Tips for a Fair Value

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: To trade in a car in the Philippines and get a fair value: To get a fair trade-in value, prepare your documents (OR/CR, IDs and, if financed, the loan release), clean the car and fix small issues, bring service records, and know your car's market range before the appraisal. Compare the trade-in offer against selling it yourself, counting the time and hassle saved.

Panahon na para mag-upgrade? Your current car can help pay for the next one. Here's how to prepare so you get a fair value and a smooth process.

How a trade-in works

  1. Appraisal

    Your car is inspected and given a value based on condition, mileage, model and market demand.

  2. Offer

    You receive an offer. Ask how it was computed.

  3. Paperwork

    Deed of sale, documents and, if there's a loan, settling the balance.

  4. Applied to your new car

    The agreed value can go toward your new car. Message me to check if a trade-in applies to your purchase and how it works with the current promo.

Prepare your documents

  • Original OR/CR, with the registration up to date
  • Valid IDs of the registered owner
  • If financed: your bank's payoff amount, and later the release of chattel mortgage
  • Service and repair records
  • Spare key, owner's manual and accessories

Prepare the car

  • Clean it inside and out: first impressions affect appraisals
  • Fix the small things: bulbs, wipers, minor warning lights
  • Don't over-invest: big repairs rarely pay back in trade-in value
  • Be honest about accidents or flood history. It always comes out in inspection

Know your car's value

Before the appraisal, check listings of the same model, year, variant and mileage to get a realistic range. Remember that listing prices are asking prices, not selling prices.

Trade-in vs selling it yourself

  • Trade-in: one transaction, less paperwork, no strangers test-driving your car
  • Selling privately: possibly a higher price, but more time, haggling and risk
  • Compare the net amount and the time and hassle saved, not just the headline offer

Frequently asked questions

Can I trade in a car that still has a loan?

Often yes, but the remaining loan balance must be settled as part of the process. Ask your bank for the exact payoff amount first.

What documents do I need to trade in a car?

Usually the original OR/CR, valid IDs, and a deed of sale. If the car was financed, you'll need the release of chattel mortgage once the loan is paid.

Does a dealer trade-in pay less than selling privately?

Sometimes, but trade-in saves you time, safety risks and paperwork. Compare the net amount and the hassle before deciding.

Do service records really matter?

Yes. Complete PMS records show the car was maintained and help support the appraisal.
