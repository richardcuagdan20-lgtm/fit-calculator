Paano Mag Car Loan: Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

By Richard Cuagdan, Mitsubishi Sales Executive · 2026-10-22

Quick answer: Paano mag car loan: alamin ang budget (monthly na around 30–35% ng gross income), pumili ng unit at mag test drive, ihanda ang requirements, ipa-pre-check sa sales executive, at hintayin ang bank approval bago ang release. Subject to bank approval palagi.

Paano mag car loan? Mas simple kaysa sa iniisip mo. Here's the step-by-step process I walk my clients through, mula pagpili ng unit hanggang release.

Step 1: Alamin ang budget mo

Keep the monthly at about 30–35% of your gross monthly income. Halimbawa, ang Xpander GLX MT na ₱22,641 kada buwan (Zero DP) ay pasok sa gross income na around ₱64,700 hanggang ₱75,500. Try the Budget Fit Calculator.

Step 2: Pumili ng unit at mag test drive

Subukan muna bago magdesisyon. Mag-book ng test drive at isama ang pamilya.

Step 3: Ihanda ang requirements

  • Dalawang valid ID at proof of billing
  • Employed: COE with compensation, payslips o ITR
  • Self-employed: DTI/SEC, business permit, ITR o financial statements, bank statements
  • OFW: contract, payslips, remittance proof, SPA o co-borrower

Buong listahan: car loan requirements.

Step 4: Submission at bank approval

  1. Pre-check

    Ire-review ko muna ang documents mo.

  2. Submit sa bangko

    Ang bangko ang mag-e-evaluate: capacity to pay, documents, credit history.

  3. Verification

    Sagutin ang tawag ng bangko.

  4. Approval

    Kapag approved, ise-schedule ang release.

Step 5: Release

Sa release day, i-check ang unit, documents at freebies. Gamitin ang release day checklist. Kasama na sa all-in ang LTO registration, TPL insurance, 1-year comprehensive insurance (rate locked for 3 years), chattel mortgage, free labor up to 5,000 km at 3-year warranty.

Para mas mataas ang chance ng approval, basahin ang car loan approval tips.

Frequently asked questions

Paano mag apply ng car loan sa bangko?

Pumili ng unit na pasok sa budget, ihanda ang requirements, at ang dealer o sales executive ang magsa-submit sa bangko. Ang bangko ang mag-a-approve.

Gaano katagal ang car loan approval?

Depende sa bangko at sa kumpletong documents. Mas mabilis kapag kumpleto at consistent ang papeles.

Kailangan ba ng down payment?

Hindi palagi. May Zero DP all-in promos, halimbawa ang Xpander GLX AT sa ₱23,877 kada buwan (October 2026, 60 months).

Pwede ba mag car loan kahit self-employed?

Oo. Karaniwang hinihingi ang DTI o SEC registration, business permit, ITR o financial statements at bank statements.
